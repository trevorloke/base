What's inside

One list of people, and everything you do with it

Most campaigns stitch together a voter file, a texting tool, an email tool, a donation page, and a website. base.win is built so you don't have to.

  • People

    Supporters, volunteers, and donors in one place, each with a single history of every knock, call, text, email, gift, and shift.

  • Map and field

    Turfs, walk lists, and door ratings on a volunteer's phone. Knocks logged without a signal send once the phone is back online.

  • Shifts

    Schedule a week by tap. Volunteers get the details by email and text, and their walk list lands on their phone.

  • Donations

    One-time and monthly gifts, receipts, and contribution limits tracked for you.

  • Outreach

    Email and one-to-one texting to any list, plus phone banks, with quiet hours and opt-outs respected.

  • Website

    Your public site, built from sections and published from the same desk that runs everything else.
