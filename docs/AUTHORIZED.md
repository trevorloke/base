# The "Authorized by" line

One place in base.win decides what the line says and where it goes:
**Website → Settings → Who's behind the site**. It is your financial
agent's name and how to reach them, plus the translations a manager
approved under Website → Languages. Nothing is ever machine-translated.

- **While the boxes are empty, nothing shows.** No email or text is held,
  and nothing nags you.
- **When you fill them in, the line goes on everything below at once.**
  That covers the website and its other websites, newsletters, email blasts,
  press releases, text blasts, the automatic follow-up emails and texts
  (ladders), share posts, the vote guide's embed, calendar files and link
  previews. Pages and ladders in another language also carry the approved
  translation.
- **What base.win can't reach.** Emails you build inside Mailchimp, paid
  ads and anything printed outside base.win: Website → Settings shows the
  line with a Copy button so you can paste it in. Your current website and
  the emails it sends are separate: until you move it to base.win, type the
  line in that site's settings by hand.

## The "needed from" date

Website → Settings has a date box that starts empty. Empty means "not
needed yet": nothing warns. Within 14 days of the date, Today shows an
amber row if the line is still missing. On and after the date, with no line
at all, Today and the Website home show a red row, and every send screen
says so. Sending then takes one more click, "Send without it", and that
choice is written down. Nothing is ever stopped quietly. Automatic sends
(ladders, the rest of an A/B test) keep sending, and Today counts how many
went without the line. With only a name (no way to reach them), the warning
is red but nothing asks.

## Where base.win adds it

This is base.win's working classification, not legal advice. You or a
lawyer can re-class any row: tell us and it changes in one place.

| Row | What | base.win adds the line |
|---|---|---|
| `site` | Every page of the website | whenever it is filled in |
| `site-plain-public` | Coming soon, a closed website, a public lesson (/learn) | whenever it is filled in (public lessons get it now) |
| `embed` | The vote guide in another website's frame | whenever it is filled in |
| `email-list` | Newsletters, email blasts, A/B tests | whenever it is filled in |
| `email-test` | A test copy to yourself | whenever it is filled in (it shows the real email) |
| `email-ladder` | Automatic follow-up emails | whenever it is filled in |
| `sms-list` | Text blasts | whenever it is filled in |
| `sms-ladder` | Automatic follow-up texts | whenever it is filled in |
| `share-kit` | Share posts for staff and volunteers | whenever it is filled in |
| `mailchimp`, `ads`, `print` | Made outside base.win | shown with a Copy button |
| `shift`, `receipts`, `account`, `office` | Shift notices, receipts, sign-in emails and pages, office lists | never |
| `link-unknown` | The page for a link that names no campaign (not found, not valid) | never: no campaign is known there |
| `calls` | Live calls, and the empty answer to an incoming text | never |

## For you (or a lawyer) to decide

Each of these gets the line whenever it is filled in, until you say
otherwise. Some already carried it before this change (press releases and
the plain pages), so the real question is "keep or stop".

1. `site-plain-own`: a reader's own pages: email preferences, a link that
   expired, and the unsubscribe page every newsletter, email blast, press
   release and automatic email links to. The website's own preferences and
   unsubscribe pages carried the line before; the unsubscribe pages the
   emails link to get it now.
2. `ics`: calendar files people add to their own calendars. They carried
   the line before.
3. `share-card`: link previews (the card a social app draws when a page link
   is pasted).
4. `share-sheet`: a supporter's own "I signed …" share words. The supporter
   can delete it before posting.
5. `email-press`: press releases to journalists. They carried the line
   before.
6. `sms-one`: the first text a volunteer or staffer sends from the Inbox or
   the Calls card. Later replies in the same conversation never get it.
7. `email-one`: a staffer's own Gmail sent through base.win, as a signature
   line.
8. `letters`: letters to the editor. base.win never sends these, so no line
   is added unless you say so.
9. `share-kit`: each share post used to have its own "Add Authorized by"
   tick box. The box is no longer offered and every post carries the line.
   Each post's saved choice is kept, so it can come back if you say so.
10. A short form for texts (for example "Auth: …"), only with exact wording
    you or a lawyer approve. Until then texts carry the full words.
11. Automatic sends after the "needed from" date with the line still
    missing: keep sending and count each one (what happens now), or hold them
    until it is filled in?
12. The receipts' financial agent: when it differs from the website's,
    Website → Settings says so and offers "Use that here", a one-click copy.
    Should one be kept in step with the other automatically? Proposed: no.
