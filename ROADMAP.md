# base.win — what we build, and what we don't

base.win is one product: the campaign's people, map, field, money, outreach
and **website** in one place. Every action anyone takes — on a doorstep, on a
phone, or on the website — lands on one person record, on the map, in time
for the next conversation. The aim is to be the one app that wins an
election (Imagine Surrey's in 2030, and our next clients'), by doing a few
things better than anyone, not by doing everything.

This is the filter. Each idea had to pass: **does it help win, and can we do
it better than the market?** Researched against New/Mode, Qomon, Solidarity
Tech, Action Network and Action Builder (November 2026), and against what a
BC campaign will need in 2030.

## Build now

1. **One product, base.win.** No more Ground, Web or Air: the app is base.win,
   and **Website** is an area of the admin like People or Donations.
2. **The website, inside base.win.** Pages built from modules (hero, text,
   what we stand for, donate, join, petition, volunteer, openings, events,
   news, promise tracker, team, groups, Q&A, video, quote…) that an admin
   adds, removes, reorders, hides and edits; any number of pages; drafts,
   preview and publish; a theme (colours with a built-in contrast check,
   fonts, logo, corners); header, footer, announcement bar, SEO. Imagine
   Surrey's site is rebuilt as its template; new clients start from a clean
   generic one. Served by base.win itself on the campaign's own domain.
3. **One record, no re-keying.** Every website sign-up, petition, pledge,
   RSVP, volunteer form, message, gift and membership becomes a person and a
   typed action in base.win the moment it happens: matched to who's already
   on file, placed on the map, consent recorded (CASL / PIPA), the referrer
   credited, and usable as a People filter — so in a list, a send, a phone
   bank or a pass ("signed the SkyTrain petition").
4. **Online money, built in (Stripe).** Donations (once or monthly) and
   memberships paid by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay. The contribution limit
   is checked before checkout and again when the payment lands (refunded if
   it would go over); the eligibility declaration is required; the receipt
   goes out by itself; nothing is retyped by the treasurer.
5. **Speed to lead.** Every new volunteer or message goes to a named
   organizer — whoever knows them, else the captain of the turf they live
   in, else the office — with a 24-hour clock on their Today. (Fast
   follow-up is what the 2025 Mamdani field team credited, per City & State.)
6. **Automations that react to everything.** Ladders gain website triggers
   (signed, pledged, RSVP'd, volunteered, gave, joined), shift triggers
   (booked, attended, no-show), a text step and goals that end a ladder
   when the person does the thing.
7. **Two-step sign-in** for everyone who can see the voter file: required
   before the public website shares a server with it.
8. **Path to win.** The win number per race, supporters found against it,
   the weekly pace needed, and which turfs to work next — on the map.
9. **⌘K.** One keystroke to any screen, any person, any action.

## Next (in order)

- **Governed AI.** "Ask base.win" (plain words → a filter you can see and
  edit), reply triage in the texting inbox, drafting and translation that a
  person approves. Runs under the asker's permissions; every write in the
  event log; never texts or calls voters by itself.
- **Every language Surrey speaks.** Per-language versions of pages, scripts
  and messages with a named reviewer; walk and call lists matched to
  volunteers' languages.
- **Events and attendance.** Supporter-hosted events, QR check-in, reminders
  with calendar invites, attendance feeding ladders.
- **Between elections.** Write-to-council letters and batched petition
  deliveries; election cycles (2026 ratings carried into 2030 filters).
- **Supporter portal.** My actions, my referrals, renew my membership.
- **Election-day scale.** The public site as its own process behind a CDN;
  the region's map tiles cached for offline; explainable priority scores.

## Not building

- Pooled data across clients or machine-learning scores trained on other
  campaigns' supporters.
- AI that texts, calls or chats with voters on its own; call recording.
- Points, badges and public leaderboards (simple team progress only).
- A social-media listening and posting suite (Starsign is a separate product).
- A shop (merchandise above market value can count as a contribution under
  LECFA; off until the financial agent signs off), ticketed events,
  federated parent/child networks.
